Isotropic Spherical Diffusion & Invisible Blue Flame Hazard

In 0.00g microgravity, buoyant upward convection vanishes (Ri = 0). Oxygen reaches the fuel solely via molecular diffusion, forming a soot-free spherical blue flame. Without incandescent soot, optical smoke detectors and human eyes can miss early cabin fires.
