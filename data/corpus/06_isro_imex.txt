SOURCE: ISRO IMEx-2026 (Indian Microgravity Experiments).
In January 2026, ISRO released the IMEx-2026 announcement for experiments aboard the Gaganyaan missions and the future Bharatiya Antariksh Station (BAS).
"Combustion and Fire Safety" is explicitly listed as a priority area.
ISRO aims to study flame spread in microgravity to design safe habitats for Indian astronauts.

SOURCE: ISRO Space Applications Centre & IIT Madras.
ISRO researchers have published microgravity combustion studies, including work on flame spread over thin circular ducts using drop towers at IIT Madras.
This confirms ISRO's strategic need for predictive fire safety tools for their upcoming LEO (Low Earth Orbit) missions.
